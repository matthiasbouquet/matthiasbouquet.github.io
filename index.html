The problem

If your results are inconsistent, it’s probably not effort you’re missing.

Most retail traders are taught entries first and risk later. Institutional desks do the opposite: define risk, size the position, then decide if the trade is worth taking.

No repeatable framework

Random setups create random P&L. You need a process for market selection, structure, entry, exit, and review.

Risk is an afterthought

Defined-risk structures, capital allocation, and trade sizing are what keep you in the game long enough to compound.

Too much noise

Greeks, IV, skew, events, macro — you only need the few inputs that actually change the trade decision.

The coaching program

Options Trading Mastery

A personalized 8-week coaching program for traders who want an institutional-style process. The roadmap below is indicative and adapts to your experience, capital, and goals.

Weeks 1–2

Foundations + options decoded

  • Options market mechanics, calls/puts, multi-leg structures
  • Institutional mindset, probability, and payoff analysis
  • Pricing, Greeks, IV vs HV, and skew without the fluff
  • Your personal trading blueprint and first demo trades

Weeks 3–4

Live process + global markets

  • Trade journaling, entry/exit frameworks, and debriefs
  • Margin requirements, capital management, and sizing
  • Futures mechanics, delta-hedging, and cross-asset context
  • How to read and stress-test trading signals

Weeks 5–6

Defined-risk structures + psychology

  • Iron condors, butterflies, calendars, and when to use them
  • Structure selection by expiry, risk, and capital allocation
  • Institutional-style risk frameworks and trade sizing
  • Emotion-proofing your process before size increases

Weeks 7–8

Advanced tactics + independence

  • Events and earnings: how to trade them, and how not to
  • Trading skew and flies with defined risk
  • Performance review, personal checklist, and scaling plan
  • Ongoing support options after the program
